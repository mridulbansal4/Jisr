## Round 1 — Survivors
1. C09 **Lapse-to-Comeback Engine** — JITAI (just-in-time adaptive intervention): detects declining engagement or wearable drift, classifies the likely barrier (COM-B), offers a smaller "minimum viable habit", never resets progress (a "comeback streak").
2. C09 **IHLAD Programme Copilot** — the coach-side console for the 24-week programme: who is lapsing, which BCT to try next, with the evidence label for each technique (BCT Taxonomy v1).
3. C10 **Stepped-Care Navigator** — PHQ-9/GAD-7 Arabic/English with change-over-time (reliable change), deterministic crisis gate (PHQ-9 item 9 or crisis language → immediate human handoff), stepped match to self-help / community / primary care / tele-MH / specialist, with eligibility by insurance.
4. C10 **Positive-screen conversion tracker** — closes the gap between positive screen and specialist follow-up (C05 pattern applied to mental health).
5. C09/C10 **Workplace wellbeing with a firewall** — aggregate-only employer insights.

## Round 2 — Highlights

- **Graham:** both are crowded; differentiation must be visible in 45 s. The C10 conversion tracker is the least crowded angle (screening-to-care for mental health, matching the C05 owner's language).
- **Thiel:** C09's secret is recovery after a lapse, not adherence — the statement literally says "without resetting progress to zero".
